How reports work

Reports are about one message, not a whole conversation. The reporting member is not named to the member who wrote the message. “Remove this message” erases its text and leaves a short note for everyone in the conversation. “Turn off messaging” lets that member read their messages but not send any. Decisions are recorded in the site log, never the message text.
