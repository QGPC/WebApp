Maximum Workshop Subsidy

The most the Guild will put toward one workshop, as a percent of that workshop’s total cost. The Program Chair sees the dollar amount on the Workshop Fee Analysis. Not shown to members.

A number from 0 to 100. For example, 20 means the Guild will cover up to one fifth of a workshop’s total cost.

One fee for the whole calendar year. It applies to every workshop held that year.
