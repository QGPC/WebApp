Membership renewal

When on, the renewal period is open and members see a Renew Membership button on their home page. Turn it off when renewals are finished.
Membership year: January 1 to December 31. Renewals are accepted until March 31 (the grace period). On April 1, members who have not renewed are marked Inactive, whether or not this button is on. You approve every renewal on Membership Requests.
