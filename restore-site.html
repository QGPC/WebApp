Restore from a Backup

Put the site back the way a saved backup had it. Superuser only.

Back to Site Administration

A restore replaces what is on the site now

Everything added after the backup was made (members, notices, registrations and so on) is lost. Only use a restore when information was lost or badly damaged and cannot be fixed by hand. For one deleted item, ask the chair to enter it again. To clear test information before launch, use Reset the Site instead.

The site is in maintenance mode

Members are seeing the “being restored” page. If no restore is running (for example, one was interrupted), turn it off so members can use the site again.

How a restore works

  1. Choose the backup file and enter its password. Nothing is changed yet.
  2. Read what the restore would do.
  3. The site first saves a safety copy of itself as it is now (guild-safety-….zip). This cannot be skipped.
  4. Tick the box, type RESTORE and enter your own password.
  5. Members see a “being restored” page for a few minutes while the information is put back. If anything fails, nothing changes and the site goes back to how it was.
  6. Do the after-restore checks below.

1. Choose the backup

Tell the Administrators and the President a restore is about to happen, and make sure no one is in the middle of important work.

3. Confirm and restore

When you press the button the site first saves a safety copy of itself as it is now (guild-safety-….zip, locked with the same password as the backup you chose). Your browser saves it to your Downloads folder or asks where. Keep it: restoring that file undoes this restore.

The site has been restored

After a restore (checklist)

  1. Sign out, then sign in as a member and as a chair. Members whose sign-in account no longer exists use “Forgot password” or ask for a first sign-in link.
  2. Look at a few records you know were in the backup: a member, a program, a notice.
  3. Check Zeffy for payments made after the backup date. The site does not know about them; mark them by hand.
  4. Tell the chairs and members what happened and what they may need to enter again (renewals, registrations, messages since the backup date).
  5. Make a new backup on the Backups page so the restored site is saved.
  6. Write down what caused the problem and what was done.
