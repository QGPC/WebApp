Your information and privacy

The Guild keeps your name, email address and membership details, the contact details and interests on this page, your photo if you add one, and any workshop sign-ups you make. Signed-in members can see your name, contact details, birthday (month and day only), interests and photo in the Member Directory, unless you have unticked the Directory box above. The Membership Chair, Administrators and the Superuser can see your whole membership record. Your password is private. To ask us to correct your details, or to have your record removed from the site, contact the Membership Chair. The Guild’s contact details are at the bottom of every page.
