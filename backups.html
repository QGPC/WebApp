Reminders

When a backup is due, the Guild Home page shows a “Time for a backup” card, and an email goes to every Superuser and Administrator on the day it is due and again every week until a backup is made. A backup made by anyone clears the reminder for everyone.

Where to keep the password

Put each backup’s password in the Guild’s password manager right away. Superusers and Administrators can both reach it. Never send a password or a backup file by email or text, and do not keep them in a personal account.
