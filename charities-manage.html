    Who can use this page

    The President, Vice President, Administrators and the Superuser add, change, reorder and remove charities. Each calendar year has its own list, so pick the year first; a new year can start from a copy of the year before. Visitors and members both read the list on the Charities We Support page. This is separate from the Organizations we support card on Comfort Quilts, which the Comfort Quilts Chair keeps.
